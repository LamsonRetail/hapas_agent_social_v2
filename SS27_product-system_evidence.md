# SS27 product-system evidence — luxury-to-commercial benchmarks

**Method.** This is a *signal audit*, not a claim that any house has announced an SS27 product plan. Store navigation/product taxonomy is current-assortment evidence; Vogue runway reviews are editorial evidence of SS26. “Not evidenced” means it was not substantiated in the sources checked—not that the brand never offers it. This distinction matters especially where official sites were inaccessible.

## SS27 cross-brand takeaways that are actually supported
1. **Build a bag architecture, not isolated SKUs:** Gucci and Longchamp most clearly demonstrate named-family + multiple use-case formats; Saint Laurent’s taxonomy supports the same ladder.
2. **Make codes legible in the product name and shop path:** Gucci’s named code families; Saint Laurent’s named franchises; Fendi’s Baguette/Peekaboo runway continuity; and Longchamp’s Le Pliage all support this.
3. **Modularity is commercially evidenced most directly at Longchamp** (straps, ribbons, scarves, charms and customisation all explicitly navigable). Gucci also supports personalisation plus charms/scarves, but source does not establish universal interoperability.
4. **Use SLG and gifts as a deliberate entry/occasion layer:** explicit at Gucci, Saint Laurent and Longchamp; Coach at least visibly has wallets. Do not extrapolate a full gift program to Coach, The Row, Fendi or Dior from this audit.
5. **Avoid false precision:** current stores and SS26 runway evidence can inform SS27 hypotheses; they do not confirm SS27 assortments, launch dates, services or market availability.
