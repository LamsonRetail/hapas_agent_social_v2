## SS27 strategic read (evidence-led, not a forecast)

1. **White space is not “another black work tote.”** Black/brown/cream dominate all observable assortments. Differentiate via controlled seasonal colour capsules and material/hand-feel, while retaining these neutrals as core colours.
2. **Silhouette baseline to meet:** compact shoulder/hoho, top-handle, soft tote, structured/trapezoid, crossbody and mini are already pervasive. A HAPAS/MATE MADE range needs a distinctive functional or material proposition rather than merely category coverage.
3. **Monetise the object ecosystem:** charms/keyrings, alternate straps, pouches, wallets/card cases and gift packaging are explicitly merchandised by all four (especially Vascara/C&K/Pedro). For SS27, plan bag + attachable personalisation + gift-ready packaging as an intentional architecture.
4. **Price-ladder implication:** Juno supplies accessible current bag examples below/around ₫1.1m; Vascara occupies observed ₫1.1–2.5m with a real-leather step-up; C&K and Pedro establish upper-mid observed prices from ~₫2.1m to ₫4.6m. Treat these as directional anchors only until an SKU-level full-catalog price audit is run.

## Limitations / failures

- The requested Juno collection URL `https://juno.vn/tui-xach` returned 404 to browser/web_scrape, though web_crawl’s sitemap layer located product URLs and the Juno homepage/product page worked. Juno category conclusions therefore rely on its live navigation, homepage observation and a single verified product page—not a complete handbag PLP.
- Vascara web_crawl returned a 100-SKU **capped sample** and combined `html_gia+sitemap_sp+bu_anh`; the crawler flags html-derived price matching as potentially imperfect. The live official PLP independently verified the displayed products/filters used above. The guessed URL for the Vascara accessory/gift page returned 404; only its live bag-page navigation supports the accessory/gift taxonomy claim.
- Charles & Keith crawler did not produce a reliable catalog extraction, but the dynamic official PLP and accessories page rendered reliable counts, filters and visible SKUs.
- Pedro crawler could not parse product cards, but dynamic official women’s and men’s PLPs rendered their counts, filters and visible SKUs. The extraction text was truncated after listed examples, so no total price-range claim is made.
- All evidence is a single current storefront observation; promotional prices, stock, SKU counts and site copy are volatile. No SS27 launch/calendar claims were inferred from the current 2026-labelled site content.
